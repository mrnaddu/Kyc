Ration Benefits & Shop Guide

Government of Karnataka • Food & Civil Supplies

Monthly Ration Entitlements

  • • 5 kg Free Rice per member per month under Central NFSA / PMGKAY.
  • • Anna Bhagya Scheme: Additional 5 kg rice OR direct monthly cash transfer of ₹170 per member into Head of Family's bank account.
  • • Antyodaya (AAY) Cards: 35 kg food grains per card (30 kg rice + 5 kg wheat) at ₹0, plus 1 kg sugar at ₹13.50/kg.

Where to Get Your Ration

  • • Distribution Period: 1st through 20th of every month.
  • • Shop Hours: Morning 7:00 AM – 12:00 PM & Evening 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM.
  • • Collection Rule: Any adult member listed on the card can collect by placing their finger on the e-PoS device.
  • • One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC): You can lift your monthly foodgrains from ANY Fair Price Shop across Karnataka using Aadhaar biometric authentication.
Locate Nearest Fair Price Shop (Maps)

Linked Government Schemes

  • • Gruha Lakshmi: ₹2,000/month for woman head of family.
  • • Ayushman Bharat - Arogya Karnataka: Up to ₹5,00,000/year free cashless treatment in government & private hospitals using this Ration Card.
  • • PM Ujjwala / Anila Bhagya: Free LPG gas connection & refill subsidies.
  • • SSP Student Scholarships: Educational fee concessions & free hostel admissions.
